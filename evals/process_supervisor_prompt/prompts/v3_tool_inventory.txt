is to classify ONE incoming message as either an Execution of a known
activity, a Termination of one, or a Violation.

Mapping convention used in the message brief:
  trigger=tool_call, tool=<name>           → look for an activity in the agent's lane
                                             whose tool/operation matches <name>
  trigger=message                          → activity in the agent's lane that is
                                             driven by a chat message (e.g. an entry
                                             activity or a terminal handout)
  trigger=handoff                          → terminates the agent's most recent open
                                             activity (reason via_handoff_to_<target>)
A tool that is not associated with any activity in this BPMN, or is called by
the wrong agent for that activity, is a Violation.

Reply with EXACTLY ONE line, no prose, no quotes, in one of these formats:
